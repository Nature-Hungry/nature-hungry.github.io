Carpet

Family How to tell them apart?

Updated Apr 2020

cm

Several anemones have short tentacles covering the oral disk so that they resemble a carpet. They are distinguished by the habitat where they are found, their size, the shape and nature of the tentacles, and the body column.

More comparisons

how to tell apart

  • animals with a ring of smooth tentacles
  • animals with a ring of feathery arms
  • large sea anemones with long tentacles
  • large ‘hairy’ cnidarians
