Mangrove jingle shell Enigmonia aenigmatica


Under-a-stone jingle shell
awaiting identification

      About 3cm, Shell thin elongated, white, golden to purplish. Found stuck to mangrove leaves, tree trunks, roots and other hard surfaces. Commonly seen in our mangroves. 2- 3cm, one side stuck to a rock, pearly, thin shell. Commonly seen on some of our shores.

Thorny oysters Spondylus sp.

3-4cm. One side of shell stuck to hard surface. Shell thick with long hollow spikes. Attached to hard surfaces. Common on all our shores. 8-15cm. Plain shell, shell opening close to the surface, not regularly zig-zag. Common on rocks on the Northern shores. 3-8cm. Shell opening away from the surface, regularly zig-zag. Common rocks on the Southern shores.   8-10cm. Lower valve is cup-shaped and stuck to a hard surface. Upper valve flat covered with flat short spines. Among living reefs. Sometimes seen on some of our shores.

These animals are NOT bivalves
