---

**Swamped
by swimming crabs?**   
Several different kinds of swimming
crabs can be found on our shores.   
Here's more on
how to tell them apart.

---

![](pelagicus/050622cftg8190m3.jpg)
![](pelagicus/050622cftg8190m3a.jpg)
![](pelagicus/050622cftg8190m3b.jpg)

**[Flower
crab](pelagicus.htm)**  
(*Portunus pelagicus*)  
Body fan-shaped

Nine 'teeth on the side of the   
body shell, the last one is   
enlarged into a long spine.

Eyes
are not far apart.

---

![](scylla/070716chgd4233m3.jpg)

![](scylla/070716chgd4233m3b.jpg)

![](scylla/070716chgd4233m3d.jpg)

[**Mud
crab**](scylla.htm)   
(*Scylla* sp.)  
Body fan-shaped

Nine
'teeth' on the side of   
the body shell, all of equal length

Eyes
not far apart

---

![](purpleleg/050621skdg7959m3.jpg)

![](purpleleg/050621skdg7959m3b.jpg)

![](purpleleg/050621skdg7959m3c.jpg)

[**Purple-legged
swimming crab**](purpleleg.htm)  
(*Charybdis hellerii*)   
Body fan-shaped

Six
'teeth' on the side of the   
body shell, all of equal length.

Eyes
are not far apart.

---

![](spinimana/050623cjg8260m3.jpg)

![](spinimana/050623cjg8260m3c.jpg)

![](spinimana/050623cjg8260m3b.jpg)

[**Red
swimming crab**](spinimana.htm)  
(*Thalamita spinimana*)  
Body is rather rectangular.

Five'
teeth' on the   
side of the body shell.

The
eyes are very wide apart

---

![](blue/041019labd7612m3.jpg)

![](blue/041019labd7612m3c.jpg)

![](blue/041019labd7612m3b.jpg)

[**Blue
swimming crab**](blue.htm)  
(*Thalamita danae*)  
Body rather rectangular

Five 'teeth' on the side of the body shell, body hairy which traps sand.

The
eyes are wide apart

---

*![](mottled/100613sngd5595m3.jpg)*

*![](mottled/100613sngd5595m3a.jpg)*

![](mottled/100613sngd5595m3d.jpg)

**[Mottled swimming crab](mottled.htm)**  
(*Thalamita* sp.)  
Body rather rectangular

5 'teeth' on the side of the body shell, tooth number four much smaller than the other teeth. Body not hairy.

The
eyes are wide apart

---

![](bluespined/040818sntd4739m3.jpg)

![](bluespined/040818sntd4739m3b.jpg)

![](bluespined/040818sntd4739m3c.jpg)

[**Blue-spined
swimming crab**](bluespined.htm)  
(*Thalamita prymna*)  
Body rather rectangular

Five
'teeth' on the side of the body shell, tooth number four much smaller than the other teeth. Body not hairy.

The
eyes are very wide apart
