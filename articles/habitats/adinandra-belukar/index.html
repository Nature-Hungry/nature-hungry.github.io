Why the Name?

Adinandra belukar means “Adinandra bush”.

  • Fragraea fragrans
  • Campnosperma auriculatum
  • Macaranga gigantea
  • Macaranga heynei

Animals Commonly Found Here

  • Asian Glossy Starling
  • Banded Woodpecker
  • Greater Racket-tailed Drongo
