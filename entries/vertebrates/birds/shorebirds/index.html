Shorebirds and sea birds

Kingdom Animalia

Updated Oct 2016

Where seen?

These rather drab but very interesting birds can be seen on and flying over our mudflats and sandflats especially during the migratory season of September to March. These are shorebirds visiting from afar. They are sometimes also called waders as they are often seen wading in water.
