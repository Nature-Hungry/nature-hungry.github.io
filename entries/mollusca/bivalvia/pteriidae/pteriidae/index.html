Winged oysters

Family Pteriidae

Updated May 2020

1.0 - 8.0 cm

Where seen?

These strangely shaped clams grow attached to other animals like sea fans and sponges, as well as mangrove trees.

What are winged oysters? Winged oysters belong to the Family Pteriidae.

Features

1-8cm. Unlike other bivalves, they have comb-like teeth in the hinge, a feature of this family. They stick to hard surfaces by byssus threads.

Human uses

Some species are collected for food by coastal populations and used as a substitute for true oysters. The oysters that produce commercial precious pearls belong to this family, but those seen on our shores do not produce pearls.
