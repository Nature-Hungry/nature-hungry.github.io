Discrepant venus clam

Gafrarium dispar*

Updated Nov 11

4.0 - 6.0 cm

Where seen?

This clam is sometimes seen on sandy shores. Elsewhere, they are found on intertidal shores with sand.

Features

About 4-6cm. Shell thick with fine ribs and usually a pattern of fine broken lines.

*

Tuas, May 05*

**Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination.

On this website, they are grouped by external features for convenience of display.*
