Sponge barnacle

Membranobalanus longirostrum

Updated Mar 2020

0.5 cm

Where seen?

The tiny feathery feet of this barnacle is sometimes seen in Chocolate sponges.

Features

Feet 0.5cm long extended out of holes in the sponge, transparent with tiny white spots. Shell embedded in the sponge and not visible.

Pulau Hantu, May 05
Sentosa, Sep 08
Sisters Island, May 08

*Species are difficult to positively identify without examination of internal parts.
