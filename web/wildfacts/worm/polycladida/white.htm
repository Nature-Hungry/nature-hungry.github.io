White flatworm
Pseudoceros meenae
Family Pseudocerotidae
updated Feb 2020
Where seen? Sometimes seen on coral rubble and reefs on some of our Southern shores.

Features: 3-4cm long. Body plain white. On body margins are broad bands, from the inside to the outer edge: black, yellow to transparent grey at the outermost edge. Has a pair of pseudotentacles made of simple folds of the body margins.
