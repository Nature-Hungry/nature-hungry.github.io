Blue-lined flatworm
Pseudoceros concinnus*
Family Pseudocerotidae
updated Feb 2020
Where seen? This small pale flatworm elegantly edged in blue with a central blue stripe is regularly encountered on many of our shores, on coral rubble near living reefs. 'Concinnus' means 'elegant' in Latin.

Features: 2-4cm long. Body creamy-white to bluish-creamy-white. Two fine blue lines in the centre, which are usually so close to one another that they appear to be one solid blue line. In between the two fine blue lines, is the body colour. Has a pair of pseudotentacles made up of simple folded edges of the body.

Sometimes mistaken for similar flatworms. Here's more on how to tell apart small flatworms with one central line and three central lines.

Good mother: One study has found this worm to display long-term parental care.

Sentosa, May 08

Pseudotentacles simple fold.

Two fine blue lines in the centre.
Pulau Semakau, Sep 05

*Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination.
On this website, they are grouped by external features for convenience of display.
