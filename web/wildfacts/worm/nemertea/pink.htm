worms > Phylum Nemertea
Pink ribbon worm
 awaiting identification*
updated Feb 14
Where seen? This long pink ribbon worm is commonly seen at night on silty areas among seagrasses and near mangroves.

Features: Grouped in this entry are ribbon worms that are 20-50cm long with broad flat bodies coloured pink or reddish. Often the head and tail paler with dark or black tip. Sometimes, one is seen swimming vigorously in the water.
