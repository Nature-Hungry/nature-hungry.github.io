Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination of internal parts.
On this website, they are grouped by external features for convenience of display.
Northern shores
 pgl: punggol, prp: pasir ris park, chg: changi, cj: chek jawa including pulau ubin, skd: pulau sekudu,
bb: beting bronok, tus: tuas, ecp: east coast park, tm: tanah merah
Southern shores
 lab: labrador, snt: sentosa, cyr: cyrene reef, kus: kusu island, laz: lazarus island, sji: st. john's island, sis: sisters islands,
jng: pulau jong, tkr: pulau tekukor, hnt: pulau hantu, tpg: terumbus pempang darat, tengah and laut, smk: pulau semakau,
tsm: submerged reefs off semakau - terumbu raya, beting bemban besar, terumbu bemban, terumbu semakau, rfl: raffles lighthouse, lfa: live firing area (Salu, Sudong, Pawai, Senang, Biola and submerged reefs in the area)
