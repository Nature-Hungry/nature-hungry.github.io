Phylum Porifera
'Century egg' sponge
Aaptos suberitoides*
Family Suberitidae
updated Oct 2016
Where seen? This black often oval-shaped sponge is bright yellow on the inside, reminding us of the 'Centure egg'. It is sometimes seen on our Southern shores. It is probably often overlooked.

Features: Oval or spherical, 10-15cm in diameter. Often partially buried in the sand. Surface lumpy, looks smooth but is rough to the touch. Holes tiny on low humps. Colour where exposed to sunlight is yellow-black, it is bright yellow on the inside.

Terumbu Pempang Tengah, Apr 12

Terumbu Pempang Laut, Jun 15
