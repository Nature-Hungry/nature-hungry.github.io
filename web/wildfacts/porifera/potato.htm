Phylum Porifera
Potato sponge
Biemna sp.*
Family Desmacellidae
updated Jul 2020
Where seen? Like a potato with eyes, this buried sponge is found on some of our shores near reefs.

Features: Spherical, oval or irregular lumpy, 10-30cm, usually buried with only chimneys sticking out of the surface. Chimneys about 1cm in diameter, 1-2cm tall. The surface is uneven and very hairy which traps sediments and sand, camouflaging the sponge. Colour pale brown.

Terumbu Pempang Tengah, Jul 10

Terumbu Hantu, Apr 12

Beting Bronok, Jun 12

*Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination.
