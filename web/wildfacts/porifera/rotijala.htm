Phylum Porifera
'Roti jala' sponge
Terpios sp.*
Family Suberitidae
updated Oct 2016
Where seen? This bright orange jumble of tangled string-like sponge does indeed resemble a local favourite Roti Jala. It is sometimes seen on Beting Bronok.

Features: Bright orange long skinny strings about 0.2cm in diameter, in a tangled jumble that may be 20cm wide. On rubbly areas or tangled on other encrusting organisms. Although the surface may appear smooth, it is rather rough. The strings are fragile.
