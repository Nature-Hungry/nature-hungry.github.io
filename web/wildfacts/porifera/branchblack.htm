Phylum Porifera
Black antler sponge
Haliclona sp.*
Family Chalinidae
updated Oct 2016
Where seen? This black sponge that resembles antles is sometimes seen on our Northern shores, growing on hard surfaces, including pipes and abandoned drums near the mid-water mark.

Features: Sparsely branching 'stems' (up to 20cm long) that are cylindrical thick (about 3-4cm in diameter) with a smooth surface. Some branches taper to elegant points. Also with low knobs. Holes at the top of knobs, and along the length of the 'stems'. Colour usually a deep black, but when exposed out of water may be greyish.

Changi, Jul 07

Pulau Sekudu, Aug 05

Changi, Jul 10

*Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination.
