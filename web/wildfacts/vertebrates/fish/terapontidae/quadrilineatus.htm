Trumpeter perch
Pelates quadrilineatus
Family Terapontidae
updated Oct 2020
Where seen? This silvery fish with a dot-dash pattern is sometimes seen on our Northern shores among seagrasses.

Features: About 8cm, up to 30cm. Adults have a silvery body with 4-6 dark horizontal lines, a black blotch behind the head and below the start of the dorsal fin and another in front of the dorsal fin. Tail fin pale or dusky without lines. Often seen in schools in brackish waters and estuaries. It croaks when taken out of water, thus its common name. Juveniles have 6-7 greyish vertical bars across the horizontal lines.

Chek Jawa, Apr 03
Changi, Jul 08
What does it eat? It eats small fishes and other invertebrates
