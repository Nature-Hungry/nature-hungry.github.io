Oyster blenny
Awaiting identification
Family Blenniidae
updated Sep 2020
Where seen? This small elongated fish is sometimes seen hidden in crevices on encrusted rocks, seawalls and rubble, even out of water at low tide.

 Features: About 5cm. Body elongated flattened sideways, head rounded. Fine white lines along the body length.
