Leaf-like fishes
How to tell them apart?
updated Sep 2020
Several different kinds of fishes are look and behave like leaves. They are flat, disk-shaped. Some have patterns that resemble dead leaves, others resemble seagrass leaves. Some may also drift with the currents with other dead leaves and other flotsam. Here's more on how to tell them apart.
