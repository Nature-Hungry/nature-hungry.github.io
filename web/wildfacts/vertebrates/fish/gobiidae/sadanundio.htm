Grey knight-goby
Stigmatogobius sadanundio
Family Gobiidae
updated Sep 2020
Where seen? This large greyish goby with spots is sometimes seen in our mangrove streams.

Features: About 6cm long. Pearly grey with 3-4 rows of small round black spots on the sides. Spots and streaks also on the fins and tail.

What does it eat? According to FishBase, it feeds on small fishes and invertebrates, including mosquito larvae.
