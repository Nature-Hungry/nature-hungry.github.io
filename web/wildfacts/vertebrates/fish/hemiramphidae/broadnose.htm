Broad-nosed halfbeak
awaiting identification*
Family Hemiramphidae
updated Sep 2020
Where seen? This small stick-like fish with a wide flattened lower jaw is commonly seen on many of our shores. Among seagrass areas, coral rubble and reefs. It is more active at night.

Features: About 6-8cm long. Body long, cylindrical and silvery. Sometimes with two broad pale or white patches, one near the head and another near the tail. The lower jaw is broad with a more rounded tip (not so pointy and sharp) and with a wide black or dark outer margin. Dorsal fin blackish. Tail fin is not forked.

The fishes grouped here may be different species.
