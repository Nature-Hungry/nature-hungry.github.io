Strapweed filefish
Pseudomonacanthus macrurus*
Family Monacanthidae
updated Sep 2020
Where seen? This filefish is sometimes seen on some of Southern shores among seagrasses and among seaweeds on the coral rubble. It is also called the Smallspotted leatherjacket.

Features: To 24cm, those seen about 10cm. Body with lots of small circular dots and often a broad white stripe or blotch along the centre of the body from the gill opening. May have dark blotches. More on how to tell apart filefishes.

Cyrene Reef, May 08
Pulau Hantu, Jul 08
