Seagrass filefish
Acreichthys tomentosus*
Family Monacanthidae
updated Sep 2020
Where seen? This filefish with with a 'smiley' on the side of the body is commonly seen on many of our shores, among seagrasses and seaweeds on coral rubble.

Features: To 12cm, those seen about 7cm. An irregular curved yellow or white line along the front of the body from the gill opening to the middle of the upper body, that resembles a 'smile'. Body in a wide variety of colours and patterns, usually irregular blotches. Adult males have bristles near the tail fin arranged a well-defined central oblong patch. More on how to tell apart filefishes.

What does it eat? It feeds on small creatures that live on the bottom such as amphipods, worms and snails.
