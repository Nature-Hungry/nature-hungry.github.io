Tiny brown porcelain crab
awaiting identification*
Family Porcellanidae
updated Dec 2019
Where seen? This tiny porcelain crab is commonly seen on our Northern shores. Often many of these little animals can be seen under stones, scurrying away rapidly when the stone is lifted. After having a look under a stone, be sure to replace the stone the way you found it. Do it carefully so you don't crush any living things.

Features: Body width less than 1cm, some can be much tinier. Body is somewhat rectangular or squarish. The body and legs are smooth, not hairy. Pincers about the same size or slightly bigger than the body. Usually a mottled grey or brown.

Different species of porcelain crabs probably match this description. Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination. On this website, they are grouped by external features for convenience of display.
