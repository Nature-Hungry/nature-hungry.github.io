Tiny colourful fiddler crab
awaiting identification*
Family Ocypodidae
updated Dec 2019
Where seen? This fiddler crab with red eyes on a dark eye stalk and a rather short and stout enlarged pincer is seen in the back mangroves.

Features: Body width 1-1.5cm. Body somewhat triangular. Red eyes on a pale or yellow eye stalk.
