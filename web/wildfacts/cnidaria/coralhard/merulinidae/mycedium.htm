Elephant-nose plate coral
Mycedium elephantotus*
Family Merulinidae
updated Nov 2019
Where seen? This hard coral forms a thick plate with large bumps. It is rarely encountered on the intertidal.

Features: Colonies 20-30cm. Encrusting plate, thick. Large cup-shaped corallites (about 1.5cm) that look like a nose. Polyp tentacles are only extended at night.
Terumbu Bemban, Apr 16

Terumbu Bemban, Jun 19

*Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination.
On this website, they are grouped by external features for convenience of display.
