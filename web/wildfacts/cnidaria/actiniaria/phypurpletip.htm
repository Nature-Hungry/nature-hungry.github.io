Purple-tip frilly anemone
Phymanthus sp.*
Family Phymantidae
updated Nov 2019
Where seen? This dark frilly anemone is seen on many of our Southern shores. Usually among coral rubble.

 Features: Diameter with tentacles extended 8-12cm. Tentacles with many fine 'branches'. Generally all the tentacles and 'branches' are same colour usually brown, greenish or greyish with violet tips. Oral disk pale or white when contracted, when expanded it is dark with fine white lines radiating from the mouth.

Sisters Islands, Jan 06

Fine white lines radiating from the mouth.

Purple or violet tips.

*Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination.
On this website, they are grouped by external features for convenience of display
