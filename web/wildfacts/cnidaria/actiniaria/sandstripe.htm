Striped sand anemone
Paracondylactis hertwigi
Family Actiniidae
updated Dec 2024
Where seen? This small anemone is often seen on our Northern shores, on soft silty sand near seagrasses.

Features: Diameter with tentacles 3-4cm. It has about 40 semi-transparent tentacles that taper to a pointed tip. On the upper side of the tentacles, there is a pattern of white bars across a pair of dark parallel lines that run the length of each tentacle. The oral disk has a pattern of radiating stripes and white bars. The edge of the mouth has dark-and-light bars. Body column smooth without large bumps (verrucae), although there are a few bumps near the oral disk.

Status and threats: As at 2024, it is assessed not to be approaching the criteria for being listed among the threatened animals in Singapore.
