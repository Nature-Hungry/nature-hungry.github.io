Polka dot anemone
Awaiting identification*
updated Nov 2019
Where seen? This small anemone with large polka dots on its body column is sometimes seen in seagrass meadows.

 Features: Diameter 3-4cm with tentacles expanded. Many tentacles, tapering translucent with tiny white spots. Body column short translucent with large white edged circular spots. Broad foot which it uses to cling onto seagrass blades.

Changi, Jun 08

Changi, Jul 12
