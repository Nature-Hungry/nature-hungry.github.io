Red berry snail
Assiminea sp.
Family Assimineidae
updated Jul 2020
Where seen? This tiny bright red round snail is commonly seen in groups on the surface of the mud in some of our back mangroves.

Features: 0.5-0.8cm. Shell thin, smooth and spherical. Colour bright red, sometimes black. Operculum thin. The body is red too, with tiny eyes on short stumpy stalks. It breathes air through a lung (instead of through gills like most other marine snails). Many members of the Family Assimineidae are adapted to brackish waters.

What does it eat? It probably grazes on the algae growing on mangrove mud.
