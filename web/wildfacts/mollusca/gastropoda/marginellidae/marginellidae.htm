Phylum Mollusca > Class Gastropoda
Margin snails
Cryptospira sp.
Family Marginellidae
updated Dec 2025
Where seen? Seen twice on sandy shores at Changi East and East Coast.

Features: About 2cm. Smooth tear-drop shaped smooth shell. The open has 'plaits' near the tip. The living animal has a large foot with mottled markings, a long siphon and eyes on stalks.

Changi East (Lost Coast), Dec 25
Photo shared by Loh Kok Sheng on facebook


East Coast (PCN), May 21
Photo shared by Toh Chay Hoon on facebook.
