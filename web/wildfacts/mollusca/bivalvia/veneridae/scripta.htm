Script venus clam
Circe scripta*
Family Veneridae
updated May 2020
Where seen? This clam is sometimes seen in sandy shores near seagrasses. Elsewhere, they are found on intertidal shores with sand.

Features: About 4-6cm. Shell rather circular, flat, thick with fine concentric ridges. Colour white with various brown lines, often zig-zag patterns.

Tuas, May 07

Pulau Hantu, May 09

*Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination.
On this website, they are grouped by external features for convenience of display.
