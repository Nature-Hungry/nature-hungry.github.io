Scintilla clam
Family Galeommatidae
updated May 2020
Where seen? This tiny clam can move like snails, crawling on a foot holding the shell on its back. It is found under stones, often several together, on many of our shores.

 Features: 1-2cm. Shell oval, thin and white. The mantle sometimes covers the entire valves. When submerged, little finger-like structures appear from the mantle. Some have a wide 'foot' created from fused mantle edges. The foot holds on to a hard surface as well as to slowly creep about or 'jump' around quickly.
