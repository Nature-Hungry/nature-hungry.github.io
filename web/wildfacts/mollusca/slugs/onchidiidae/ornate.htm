Ornate onch slug
awaiting identification*
Family Onchidiidae
updated Jun 2020
Where seen? This small oval slug with an ornate texture is sometimes seen in mangroves in the North.

Features: 3-5cm. Body hard, oval, rounded. Skin with regular pattern of bumps with blunt spikes on them, resulting in a very ornate texture! Colour plain, beige or greyish. Eyes on long thin stalks. Underside and narrow foot are pale to lemon yellow.
