Nudibranch eggs
updated Apr 2020
if you learn only 3 things about them ...
Egg capsules contain living eggs or tiny snails. Don't disturb them.
Egg capsules are found on stones and among seaweeds. Watch your step.
Please don't bring egg capsules home. Thousands of baby snails may die.

Where seen? These rosette-like ribbons of eggs are sometimes seen on many shores.

Features: Diameter 8-1cm. Frilly ribbon of eggs embedded in mucus, laid in a spiral on hard surfaces or among seaweeds and seagrasses. Usually white or beige.
