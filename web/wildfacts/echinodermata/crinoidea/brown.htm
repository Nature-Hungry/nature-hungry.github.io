Brown feather star
awaiting identification
updated Sep 2019
Where seen? This brown feather star is sometimes seen near living reefs on Pulau Hantu.

Features: 12-15cm in diameter. With more than 20 arms. The cirri on the underside is banded. Included on this page are animals that are mostly brown as well as banded brown or orange.

Pulau Hantu, May 19
Photo shared by Loh Kok Sheng on facebook.

Pulau Hantu, Jun 08

Cirri on the underside
used to cling onto things.

*Species are difficult to positively identify without close examination.
On this website, they are grouped by large external features for convenience of display.
