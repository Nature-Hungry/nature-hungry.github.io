Mangrove synaptid sea cucumber
awaiting identification*
Family Synaptidae
updated Apr 2020
Where seen? This rather long sea cucumber is sometimes seen burrowing in or on mudflats or muddy areas near mangroves on our Northern shores.

Features: About 20-30cm long, long thin body without bumps along the body. Feeding tentacles short and feathery. Colours uniform white, pink, purplish with stripes.

Sometimes mistaken for worms. Here's more on how to tell apart worm-like animals.
