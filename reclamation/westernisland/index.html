Western Islands

In the 2026 National day rally, PM Lawrence Wong mentioned the plans to merge a few southern islands into one island through relcamation with the goals of building advanced manufacturing and energy infrastructure to prepare Singapore for the future.

In the center of this lies a cluster of Terumbus, reefs that are exposed during low tide, and are surrounded by lush coral reefs in the deeper water. These reefs are estimated to be over 8000 years old.

These places hold the vast majority of corals reefs, seagrass meadows and other marine ecosystems that still remain in Singapore. They serve as a stronghold for marine life that seeds other reefs in the region that help create more resilient ecosystems and recover previously destroyed habitats

Its ecological significance has been well-studied and there has been many recommendations to set up a formal marine reserve to protect these precious habitats.

We urge the government to review this decision and explore more sustainable, efficient land-use alternatives.
