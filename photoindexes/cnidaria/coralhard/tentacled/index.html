Hard corals on Singapore shores

Polyps with long tentacles often hiding a hard skeleton when polyps are extended


Brain anchor coral

Euphyllia ancora*

Colonies 10-20cm. Corallites forming meandering valleys with separate walls. Tentacles with a U-shaped tip. Colours blue and brown with bluish tips. Seen on our Southern shores.

Galaxy corals

Galaxea

Torch anchor coral

DD

Euphyllia glabrescens

Colonies 10-20cm. Corallites trumpet-shaped. Tentacles with rounded tips. Colours beige, brown and blue. Sometimes seen on our Southern shores.

Branching anchor corals

Euphyllia paraancora*

Colonies 10-20cm. Corallites trumpet-shaped. Tentacles with U-shaped tips. Colours green, brown and purple with white or bluish tips. Seen on our Southern shores.

Frog spawn coral

Euphyllia paradivisa*

Colony 10-20cm. Corallites trumpet-shaped. Tentacles are branched at the tips into masses of rounded and U-shaped tips. Colours green and brown with white or bluish tips. Rarely seen, on our Southern shores

Pearl bubble coral

VU

Physogyra lichtensteini*

Colony 10-20cm. Corallites brain-like meandering made up of large coin-like disks. In daylight covered in bubbles. Colours beige. Rarely seen, on our Southern shores
